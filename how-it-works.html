How it works

Your decision remains yours.

We make introductions simpler while keeping the commercial and clinical boundaries clear.

Three straightforward steps.

An enquiry is not consent to treatment and creates no obligation to proceed.

  1. Send a minimal enquiryProvide your name, preferred contact and broad treatment interest. Do not send sensitive medical details.
  2. Confirm an introductionWe explain the type of provider who may receive your details. You can decline.
  3. Arrange a provider consultationThe independent provider assesses suitability, risks, consent, costs and aftercare.

Questions to ask any provider

  • Who will perform or prescribe the treatment, and what are their qualifications?
  • Which professional register applies, and how can I verify it?
  • What are the material risks and realistic alternatives?
  • Where will treatment take place, and who handles complications?
  • What is included in the quoted price and aftercare?
  • What cooling-off time is available before I decide?
