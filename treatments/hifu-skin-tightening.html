What is HIFU skin tightening?

HIFU may be offered for modest facial or body skin-tightening goals. It does not reproduce surgical lifting and cannot guarantee a defined jawline. Devices vary substantially in quality and intended use.

What should you consider before treatment?

The operator should assess anatomy and avoid vulnerable structures. Ask for the exact device, treatment depths, expected timeline and why HIFU is preferred over radiofrequency, surgery or no treatment.

Risks and recovery

Pain, swelling, redness, bruising and temporary numbness can occur. Burns, fat loss, nerve injury and contour changes are possible with poor technique or unsuitable settings.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What exact device and cartridges are used?
  • What change is realistic for my concern?
  • How does the operator protect nerves and fat compartments?
  • Who manages a burn or nerve symptom?
