What is Microneedling?

Standard microneedling and radiofrequency microneedling are different treatments with different costs and risk profiles. They may be discussed for selected texture, acne-scar or skin-quality concerns, often as a course.

What should you consider before treatment?

Active infection, some inflammatory skin conditions, scarring tendency and medicines may alter suitability. Ask whether the device is single-use or appropriately sterilised and what topical products will be applied.

Risks and recovery

Redness, swelling, pinpoint bleeding, dryness and temporary sensitivity can occur. Infection, pigment change, scarring and flare-ups are possible. Deeper or radiofrequency treatment generally needs more careful assessment and downtime planning.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Is this standard or radiofrequency microneedling?
  • What needle depth and course are proposed?
  • How is sterility maintained?
  • What pigment-change risk applies to my skin tone?
