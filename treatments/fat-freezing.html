Non-surgical body treatment guide

Fat freezing and cryolipolysis in Manchester.

Cryolipolysis cools selected fat tissue to change a local contour. It is not weight-loss treatment and does not tighten loose skin.

What is Fat freezing?

Applicators are placed over selected areas and a course or multiple cycles may be recommended. Results develop gradually and vary. The treatment cannot match the amount or precision of surgical liposuction.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask which device is used, whether it is authorised for the proposed area, how many applicator cycles are included and what change is realistic for your skin and fat distribution.

Risks and recovery

Temporary numbness, bruising, tenderness and firmness can occur. Burns, contour irregularity and nerve symptoms are possible. Rare paradoxical adipose hyperplasia causes the treated area to enlarge and may require surgery.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What exact device will be used?
  • How many cycles does the price include?
  • What is the risk of paradoxical enlargement?
  • Who assesses and treats a complication?
