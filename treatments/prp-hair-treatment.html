Hair treatment guide

PRP hair treatment in Manchester.

Platelet-rich plasma uses a patient’s processed blood and is sometimes offered for selected hair-loss concerns, usually as a course.

What is PRP hair treatment?

Blood is drawn, processed to concentrate platelets and injected into the scalp. Protocols vary and evidence is not equal for every type of hair loss. A diagnosis should come before treatment.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask what condition is being treated, what evidence supports the proposed protocol, how platelet concentration is controlled and whether established medical or surgical alternatives should be considered.

Risks and recovery

Pain, bleeding, bruising, swelling and headache can occur. Infection and injury are possible if preparation or injection standards are poor. Results vary and maintenance sessions may be proposed.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What diagnosis supports PRP?
  • What preparation system and protocol are used?
  • How will response be measured?
  • What is the full course and maintenance cost?
