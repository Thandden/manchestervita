Non-surgical facial treatment guide

Thread lift treatment in Manchester.

A thread lift places absorbable threads beneath the skin for a temporary lifting effect; it is not a non-surgical equivalent of a facelift.

What is Thread lift?

Thread type, number and placement vary. Results are generally subtler and less predictable than surgery, and they fade. Skin quality, tissue heaviness and expectations affect suitability.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask what problem threads are intended to address, how long change may last and whether filler, energy treatment, surgery or no treatment is a better comparison. More threads do not guarantee a better result.

Risks and recovery

Bruising, swelling, tenderness, dimpling and thread visibility can occur. Infection, asymmetry, extrusion, nerve irritation and persistent contour problems are possible. Removal or corrective treatment may be difficult.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which thread product and technique are proposed?
  • What result and duration are realistic?
  • What happens if a thread is visible or infected?
  • How could future surgery be affected?
