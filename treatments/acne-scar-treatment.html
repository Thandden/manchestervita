What is Acne scar treatment?

Ice-pick, rolling, boxcar and raised scars respond differently. Options may include subcision, microneedling, laser, chemical reconstruction, peels, filler or surgery. Active acne and pigmentation should be assessed first.

What should you consider before treatment?

A provider should examine scar type, skin tone, active inflammation and previous treatment. Ask which scar each method targets, the likely course, downtime and how improvement will be measured without promising scar removal.

Risks and recovery

Risks vary by method and include infection, pigment change, prolonged redness, burns, scarring, filler complications and acne flare. Combining treatments can increase downtime and cost.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which scar types do I have?
  • Why is each proposed treatment included?
  • What pigment risk applies to my skin tone?
  • What improvement and course are realistic?
