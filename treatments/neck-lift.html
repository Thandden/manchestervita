Neck lift surgery in Manchester.

Neck lift surgery may address selected skin laxity, muscle bands and fat, sometimes alongside lower-face surgery.

What is Neck lift?

A plan may include liposuction, platysma muscle work and skin redraping. A neck lift cannot stop ageing or guarantee a sharply defined jawline. The surgeon should distinguish skin, fat, muscle and bone-related contributors.

What should you consider before treatment?

A neck-only operation may not address lower-face laxity, while a combined face and neck lift is more extensive. Ask where incisions will sit and what each part of the operation is intended to change.

Risks and recovery

Risks include bleeding, infection, scars, contour irregularity, altered sensation, asymmetry, nerve injury and anaesthetic complications. Swelling and tightness can persist beyond the initial social recovery period.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Is neck-only surgery likely to meet my goal?
  • Will liposuction or muscle repair be included?
  • Where are the scars?
  • What is the surgeon’s face and neck surgery experience?

How much does Neck lift cost in Manchester?

Around £7,000–£12,000

Indicative price for neck-focused surgery. Combined facelift, surgeon, anaesthesia, facility and overnight care can increase cost. This is a guide, not a quote.
