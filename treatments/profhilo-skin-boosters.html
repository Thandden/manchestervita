What is Profhilo and skin boosters?

Injectable hyaluronic-acid products may be proposed to support hydration or skin quality rather than add the projection associated with conventional filler. Products, injection patterns, licensing and protocols vary.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask for the exact product, intended effect, evidence, number of sessions and whether its proposed use is licensed. A standard protocol often involves an initial course, but more treatment is not automatically better.

Risks and recovery

Expected effects can include small bumps, redness, swelling, tenderness and bruising. Infection, inflammatory reactions and vascular complications are possible with injections. Confirm the practitioner’s emergency and follow-up plan.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What exact product will be injected?
  • How many sessions and maintenance visits are proposed?
  • What result is realistic for my skin concern?
  • Who manages an unexpected reaction?
