What is HydraFacial?

Treatment is generally intended as a temporary skin-refresh service rather than a medical solution for scarring, pigmentation or disease. Protocols and added boosters vary between providers.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask which steps and boosters are included, whether active acne or sensitive skin changes suitability and what evidence supports claims made for the package. Results should not be described as permanent.

Risks and recovery

Temporary redness, sensitivity, irritation or breakouts may occur. Hygiene and single-use consumables matter. Anyone with active infection or significant skin disease should seek appropriate clinical advice first.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What does the advertised package include?
  • Are tips and consumables single-use?
  • Is the treatment suitable for my current skin condition?
  • How long should any visible effect last?
