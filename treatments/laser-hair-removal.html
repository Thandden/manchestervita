What is Laser hair removal?

Laser hair removal targets pigment in hair follicles. It is not guaranteed permanent removal, and white, grey, red or very fine hair may respond poorly. Device selection and settings should account for skin tone.

What should you consider before treatment?

Ask whether the device is a true laser or IPL, who operates it, whether a patch test is required and how tanning or medicines affect treatment. Courses and maintenance vary by area and hair cycle.

Risks and recovery

Temporary redness and swelling are common. Burns, blisters, pigment change and scarring can occur. Poor device or setting choice increases risk, especially for darker or recently tanned skin.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which device and wavelength will be used?
  • What experience does the operator have with my skin tone?
  • How many sessions are realistic?
  • What is the patch-test and adverse-reaction process?
