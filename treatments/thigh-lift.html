Thigh lift surgery in Manchester.

Thigh lift surgery removes selected loose skin and may improve contour after weight loss, but requires substantial scars and recovery.

What is Thigh lift?

Medial and other thigh-lift patterns address different areas. The amount and location of excess skin, lymphatic health, weight stability and desired contour determine the incision and whether liposuction is added.

What should you consider before treatment?

Scars may sit in the groin, extend down the inner thigh or follow other patterns. Friction, moisture and movement can make wound healing challenging. Ask for a realistic scar and mobility plan.

Risks and recovery

Risks include bleeding, infection, seroma, wound separation, prominent scars, asymmetry, altered sensation, swelling, lymphatic problems and blood clots. Practical support and thrombosis prevention are important.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which scar pattern is required?
  • How stable should my weight be?
  • How will mobility and clot risk be managed?
  • What happens if a wound opens?

How much does Thigh lift cost in Manchester?

Indicative private price. Extent, liposuction, anaesthesia, hospital stay, garments and wound follow-up affect the quote. This is a guide, not a quote.
