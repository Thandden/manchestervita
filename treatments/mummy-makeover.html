Combined surgery guide

Mummy makeover surgery in Manchester.

A “mummy makeover” is a marketing label for a personalised combination of operations, commonly involving breast and abdominal surgery.

What is Mummy makeover?

The term may include tummy tuck, breast uplift, augmentation, reduction or liposuction. There is no standard package. Goals, previous pregnancies, weight stability, scars, childcare and future pregnancy plans should shape whether procedures are combined, staged or not pursued.

What should you consider before treatment?

Combining operations may mean one anaesthetic and recovery period, but longer operating time can increase strain and complication risk. Ask the surgeon to justify each component and explain whether staging would be safer.

Risks and recovery

Risks combine those of every included operation and may include bleeding, infection, wound problems, seroma, implant complications and blood clots. Recovery can substantially limit lifting and childcare. Arrange adult support and understand the emergency and aftercare plan.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which exact operations are proposed?
  • Would staging reduce risk?
  • How long will lifting and childcare be restricted?
  • What does the total price include if a complication occurs?

How much does Mummy makeover cost in Manchester?

Around £12,000–£20,000+

A broad indicative range because the operation is not standardised. Components, staging, theatre time, hospital stay, implants and aftercare can take costs higher. This is a guide, not a quote.
