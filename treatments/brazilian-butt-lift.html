Brazilian butt lift enquiries in Manchester.

Gluteal fat grafting, often called a BBL, transfers fat to the buttocks. It has a distinct risk profile that demands specialist safeguards.

What is Brazilian butt lift?

Fat is removed by liposuction, processed and injected to alter gluteal contour. Not all transferred fat survives, donor supply is limited and results cannot be guaranteed. Technique, injection plane and postoperative monitoring are central safety questions.

What should you consider before treatment?

UK professional bodies have issued specific safety guidance because fat entering large veins can cause fatal embolism. Price or social-media popularity should never outweigh surgeon training, ultrasound-guided technique where applicable, accredited premises and emergency planning.

Important: BBL surgery can cause fatal fat embolism. Only consider an appropriately trained surgeon working in regulated premises with a clear emergency pathway.

Risks and recovery

Risks include fat embolism, blood clots, infection, fluid collection, contour irregularity, fat necrosis, asymmetry and anaesthetic complications. Travel and sitting restrictions can complicate recovery. Overseas surgery may make urgent and long-term aftercare harder.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • What procedure-specific safety guidance does the surgeon follow?
  • How is injection plane monitored?
  • Where is surgery performed and regulated?
  • Who provides emergency and longer-term aftercare?
