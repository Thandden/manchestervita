Brow lift surgery in Manchester.

A brow lift changes selected brow position and forehead tissues; it is different from eyelid surgery and anti-wrinkle injections.

What is Brow lift?

Endoscopic, temporal and open techniques use different incisions and provide different degrees of change. Brow position, hairline, forehead height, eyelid skin and facial movement should be assessed together.

What should you consider before treatment?

Raising the brow can affect expression and hairline appearance. Ask whether the concern arises from brow descent, eyelid skin, muscle activity or a combination, and whether a less extensive option exists.

Risks and recovery

Risks include bleeding, infection, scarring, asymmetry, altered sensation, hair loss around incisions, nerve injury and an unwanted surprised appearance. Recovery varies with technique and combined procedures.

Questions to ask at a consultation

  • Which technique and incision pattern are proposed?
  • How could my hairline change?
  • Would eyelid surgery address a different concern?
  • How is asymmetry planned for?

How much does Brow lift cost in Manchester?

Around £5,000–£9,000

Indicative private price. Technique, combined eyelid work, surgeon, anaesthesia, facility and follow-up affect the quote. This is a guide, not a quote.
