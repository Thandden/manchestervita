About Manchester Vita

Information first. Introductions second.

Manchester Vita is an independent health and wellness information brand for adults considering cosmetic procedures in Greater Manchester.

On this page

Our roleOur standardsCommercial disclosure

What we do

We publish introductory treatment guides and receive enquiries from people who want to explore a cosmetic procedure. Where appropriate, an enquiry may be introduced to an independent clinic or practitioner able to discuss next steps.

We are not a clinic. Manchester Vita does not diagnose, prescribe, perform treatments or provide medical advice. A provider must conduct their own consultation and decide whether any treatment is suitable.

How information should be used

Our guides help you prepare questions; they cannot tell you which treatment to choose. Before proceeding, check the provider’s identity, qualifications, registration where applicable, facility arrangements, insurance, complication plan, aftercare and total costs.

Clinical claims and article review details should be added only when a named, appropriately qualified reviewer has completed that review.

How we may be paid

Manchester Vita may receive a fee when an enquiry is introduced to a provider. Payment does not replace your own assessment of that provider and must not determine clinical advice. Ask who will receive your details before agreeing to an introduction.
