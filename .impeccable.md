## Design Context

### Users
Adults in Greater Manchester researching surgical and non-surgical cosmetic procedures. They want clear, calm information, realistic expectations and a simple way to request contact from an independent provider.

### Brand Personality
Premium, discreet and reassuring. Manchester Vita should feel like a considered health and wellness brand: editorial rather than sales-led, polished without pretending to be a treatment provider.

### Aesthetic Direction
“Quiet northern prestige”: warm ivory, deep mineral green and restrained copper; large editorial serif typography paired with a clean sans serif; fine rules, asymmetry and generous space. Light theme only. Avoid beauty-spa clichés, gold gradients, glossy perfection, generic card grids and unsupported clinical imagery or credentials.

### Design Principles
1. Trust before conversion: clearly disclose the independent enquiry/referral model.
2. Calm clarity: plain-English treatment information, risks and next steps.
3. Editorial restraint: typography, rhythm and material-inspired colour create the premium feel.
4. Accessible by default: semantic HTML, visible focus, strong contrast and large targets.
5. Local without doorway spam: useful Manchester context, not duplicated place-name pages.
